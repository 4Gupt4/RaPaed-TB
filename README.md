# RaPaed-TB: WHO Treatment Decision Algorithm

**WHO treatment decision algorithms for childhood tuberculosis: exploring the added value of conventional and emerging tests**

This is a secondary, exploratory analysis of RaPaed-TB ([NCT03734172](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03734172)), a prospective multicentre diagnostic accuracy study of children aged 3 months to 10 years with presumptive pulmonary tuberculosis in five low- and middle-income countries. The study assesses how integrating conventional and emerging index tests at decision nodes of the WHO treatment decision algorithms (TDAs) changes their diagnostic accuracy against a composite reference standard based on the NIH 2015 clinical case definitions.

The overall aim is to improve the specificity of the TDAs and reduce overtreatment of children without tuberculosis. *WHO operational handbook on tuberculosis: Module 5: Management of tuberculosis in children and adolescents.* Geneva: World Health Organization; 2022. Available from: [https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK579387/](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK579387/). Specifically:

1. Evaluate the baseline diagnostic accuracy of the TDAs (**TDA-A**, with CXR; **TDA-B**, without CXR) and four index tests — haemoglobin (**Hb**), C-reactive protein (**CRP**), a 3-gene host-response signature (**MTB-HR**), and a T-cell activation marker assay (**TAM-TB**).
2. Assess **standalone** and **parallel** integration of index tests at four TDA decision nodes (High-risk, mWRD, TB-contact, Post-score), using inclusive (OR-rule) and restrictive (AND-rule) configurations.
3. Assess **sequential** integration of index tests across multiple decision nodes in three predefined scenarios.

Study sites contributing to RaPaed-TB:

* Cape Town, South Africa (UCT)
* Blantyre, Malawi (KUHeS)
* Vellore, India (CMC)
* Mbeya, Tanzania (NIMR-MMRC)
* Maputo, Mozambique (INS)
